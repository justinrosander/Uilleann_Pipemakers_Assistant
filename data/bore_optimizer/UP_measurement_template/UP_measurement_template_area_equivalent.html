A round probe and an oval probe of the same diameter seat at different depths. The oval probe is usually inserted further. At the oval-probe depth the minor diameter is intercepted from the round-probe curve. At the round-probe depth the major diameter is intercepted from the oval-probe curve. The design diameter is Deq = √(Dmin × Dmaj) at that station. Insertion depth locates the station. It is not the design quantity.

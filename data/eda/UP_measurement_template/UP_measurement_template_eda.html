Business context

Uilleann pipes comprise of a chanter, regulators and drones. Regulators are similar to chanters in terms of having conical bores and toneholes. Drones generally have more of a straight bore, but sometimes may have a slight taper. Measuring historical sets for the purpose of copying them involve using probes to collect data pairs: The diameter of the bore and the depth at which the diameter measurement was taken. Problems arise when copying historical sets as the bore diameter is not an ideal circle due to the expansion and contraction of wood over time. This results in an oval profile, so oval probes are also used to collect data for the major axis of the oval to collect another set of data pairs: The major axis diameter of the bore and the depth at which the diameter measurement was taken. The objective here is to provide visualizations of actual bore measurements to help a pipemaker who is copying a given set make decisions on how to approach ideal reamer design. This will involve EDA, and from the EDA observations utilizing regressions to arrive at an ideal round profile between the round and oval measurements.

Analytical objectives

  1. Provide visualizations of the actual bore measurements of each instrument: chanter, regulators and drones.
  2. Compare the bore dimensions between chanter and regulators and identify feature relationships.
  3. Evaluate comparison in each instrument where round and oval measurements were taken. From this evaluation, use linear and non-linear regressions to establish the mean between the round and oval measurements to establish an ideal, round bore profile for the purpose of turning reamers.
  4. Confirm whether dependent-independent variable relationships exist.
  5. Summarize univariate behavior of all variables.
  6. Highlight bivariate relationships for strongly correlated pairs (|r| >= 0.7).
